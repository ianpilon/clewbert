# Your agent team

Every board has a team. You can rename them, change their jobs in plain words, and add teammates under **Agents** (top right).

## Lead
Reads your task and the board, splits the work into parts, and hands each part to a teammate. When the parts are done, the Lead writes the final answer in the task's frame.

## Researcher
Searches the web, reads pages and documents, and puts what it finds on the board as notes and link cards, each with where it came from.

## Writer
Turns the findings into something you can use: a summary, a plan, an email draft. It writes from what is on the board, not from memory.

## Starters you can add
- **Analyst**: compares options and says which is best and why.
- **Fact-checker**: checks the claims on the board against their sources.
- **Developer**: reads code and builds small working demos you can open.

## How they work with you
- Each part of a task gets its own frame, joined to the Lead's by an arrow.
- When they need you, their question shows up on the board and under Tasks. Answer it there or out loud.
- They always ask before anything that sends, buys, books, posts or signs up.
