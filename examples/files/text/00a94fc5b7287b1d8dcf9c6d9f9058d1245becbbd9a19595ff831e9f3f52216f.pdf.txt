Clewbert quick guide
A brainstorming partner you talk to, with an agent team behind you.
1. Bring in anything
Paste screenshots and links, drop PDFs, documents, videos and any other file.
Clewbert reads it all, including the words inside pictures and PDFs.
2. Talk to it
Click the microphone and ask out loud: "What is on this board?"
Say "show me" or "go into" a frame, and the board moves there.
Answers come only from your boards. The items used light up.
3. Hand work to your team
Press A, say what you need, and press Start.
The Lead splits the work, teammates research, read, write and build demos.
Their questions show up on the board. They ask before sending or buying anything.
4. Keep it private
Your boards are plain files on the computer that runs Clewbert.
Pick the model for each job in Settings, including models on your own machine.
Clewbert can also hear and speak with its own free voice, inside your browser.
Handy keys
N sticky note T text A agent task M mute the microphone
Cmd+Z undo Shift+Cmd+Z redo Esc close what is open
