Welcome to Clewbert

Talk to your boards, and little helpers find the thread. Clewbert needs an AI model to think and talk. Connect one to start.

The key is saved only on the computer that runs Clewbert. Your questions, and the boards Clewbert reads to answer them, go to the provider you pick.

Running your own model? Pick Your own and type its address, or start Clewbert with CANVAS_MODEL_URL set (see the README).

You are connected

Your browser will ask to use the microphone. Clewbert listens only while the voice card is open, and M mutes it.

Shortcuts and tips
  • ⌘V paste screenshots and links · drag in any file
  • Drag empty space to select · Space+drag, two-finger scroll or middle button to pan · ⌘-scroll to zoom
  • Tools: V select · N note · T text · P marker · A agent task
  • Double-click for a note, or a note to edit it · hold ⌥ while resizing to grow from the centre
  • ⌘Z undo · ⇧⌘Z redo
  • Voice: M mutes the mic · Esc closes what is open
